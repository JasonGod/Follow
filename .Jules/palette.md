## 2026-07-18 - Tooltips and Aria-Labels for Custom Window Controls in Electron
**Learning:** Window control buttons (minimize, maximize, restore, close) in custom Electron titlebars are crucial interactive components that are frequently missed by accessibility checkers or keyboard users. Because custom titlebars often use `overflow: hidden` and are positioned at the extreme boundary of the screen, using standard tooltips can result in clipping. Additionally, custom focus ring patterns must be inset to ensure the focus border is not clipped by the viewport or parent edges.
**Action:** Always wrap custom titlebar action buttons in Tooltips using `TooltipPortal` to avoid layout clipping, provide localized `aria-label` attributes for screen readers, and style focus indicators with `focus-visible:ring-inset` to keep focus indicators fully visible.
