## 2025-02-14 - Titlebar Accessibility and Tooltip Layout
**Learning:** Standard toolbars and window control areas often use `overflow: hidden` to constrain content layouts, which causes standard custom tooltips (like Radix UI Tooltip) to get clipped or fully hidden. Additionally, interactive buttons situated at the absolute edges of custom window borders require inset focus rings (`focus-visible:ring-inset`) to prevent indicators from being truncated outside the viewport. To avoid tooltip focus-trap issues on Radix tooltips after a mouse click, focus capturing should stop propagation.
**Action:** Always wrap edge-aligned titlebar controls with `<TooltipPortal>` to render out-of-bounds, apply inset ring styles for visible keyboard focus indication, and stop focus propagation using `onFocusCapture={stopPropagation}` to maintain clean state transition on mouse interaction.
