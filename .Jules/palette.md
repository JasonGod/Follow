## 2025-01-24 - [Titlebar Accessibility & Tooltips]
**Learning:** In Electron apps with custom titlebars, window control buttons are often icon-only and lack standard accessibility features. Using Radix Tooltips within a container that has `overflow: hidden` (common for titlebars) requires `TooltipPortal` to prevent the tooltip from being clipped. Standard Windows localized terms for these actions in Simplified Chinese are: 最小化 (Minimize), 最大化 (Maximize), and 向下还原 (Restore). Additionally, `focus-visible:ring-inset` is preferred for buttons at the window edge to ensure the focus indicator remains visible and within bounds.
**Action:** Always provide `aria-label` for icon-only window controls, use `TooltipPortal` for tooltips in constrained containers, and apply `focus-visible:ring-inset` for edge-aligned interactive elements.
